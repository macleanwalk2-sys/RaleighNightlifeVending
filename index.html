The part that actually matters to you

You are not putting your ABC permit on the line for a vending machine. Age control is the entire design of this business, and the licensing and liability sit with us.

  • 21+ venues only. We place machines exclusively in age-restricted venues — never in a space where a minor could walk up to one.
  • ID verification before every sale. The machine will not dispense until a valid ID is scanned and verified at the point of purchase.
  • Licensed supply chain. Every product is sourced through licensed distributors, with documentation available to you on request.
  • Our permits, our liability. We carry the required licensing and general liability coverage, and we'll name your venue on the certificate.
  • Records on request. Sales and service history for your location, available any time you or your attorney ask for it.

What venue owners ask us first

What does this cost me?

Nothing. We own the machine and the inventory and cover installation and service. You provide the floor space and a standard outlet.

How do you keep a minor from buying?

Two layers. First, we only place machines in venues that are already 21+ at the door. Second, the machine itself requires a valid scanned ID before it will dispense anything, every single time.

What happens if it jams or goes down?

The customer contacts us directly — there's a number on the machine — and we handle the refund and the repair. Your staff should never have to touch it.

How much room does it need?

About two feet of wall. We'll find a spot during the walkthrough that doesn't cost you seating or block a path.

How and when do I get paid?

Once a month, with a report showing exactly what sold in your venue. The percentage is set in the placement agreement before anything gets installed.

What if I want it gone?

We remove it. The agreement is month-to-month with no termination fee, because we'd rather earn the spot than lock you into it.

Let's look at your space

Tell us where you are and we'll come by at a slow hour. No pitch deck, no pressure — we'll show you the machine, walk the floor, and give you the numbers in writing.
