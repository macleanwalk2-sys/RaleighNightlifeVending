Questions we get

What does this cost me?

Nothing. We own the machine and the stock. You provide wall space and an outlet.

How do you keep minors out?

21+ venues only, and the machine needs a scanned ID before it dispenses anything.

What if it breaks?

There's a number on the machine. We handle the refund and the repair, not your staff.

How much room does it need?

None on the floor. About two feet of wall, nine inches deep.

How do I get paid?
