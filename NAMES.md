# Business name candidates

`Raleigh Nightlife Vending` is the placeholder currently on the site. It's
clear and it does the job, but it's descriptive rather than memorable, and it
locks the business to one city.

## Read this before you fall in love with a name

**Keep "vape," "smoke," and "nicotine" out of the legal name.** This is the
single most practical constraint on the list. Payment processors, business
bank accounts, insurance underwriters, and Google/Meta ad platforms all treat
tobacco-adjacent businesses as high risk, and a name that announces the
category gets flagged by automated review before a human ever reads it. A
neutral name also lets you add other machines — phone chargers, ice, hangover
kits, whatever sells at 1am — without renaming the company.

**Check before committing:** the `.com`, the Instagram handle, and the
[NC Secretary of State business registry](https://www.sosnc.gov/online_services/search/by_title/_Business_Registration)
for an existing entity with the same name.

---

## Top four

**Last Call Vending** — Bar vernacular, so it lands instantly with the exact
person you're pitching. Warm, a little funny, easy to say over loud music, and
completely neutral to a payment processor. Strongest of the batch.

**Barside Vending** — The most B2B-professional option here. Describes your
placement rather than your product, which is the right emphasis for a company
whose customer is the venue, not the drinker. Reads well on a contract.

**Night Owl Vending Co.** — Friendly, memorable, and it already matches the
crescent moon mark on the site. "Co." adds a little more weight than the
business currently has, in a good way. Best name for growing beyond vapes.

**Oak City Vending** — Local without being literal. Raleigh people recognize
Oak City immediately, out-of-towners don't need to, and it quietly rhymes with
Oakwood Marketing, which makes the parent-company relationship look deliberate.

## Recommendation

**Last Call Vending** to a bar owner, **Barside Vending** if you want the
safest, most professional-sounding option on a placement agreement. Either way
register the LLC under the neutral name and treat "Raleigh Nightlife Vending"
as the descriptor in the tagline, not the brand.

Whatever you land on, the swap is a find-and-replace across `index.html`
(header, footer, `<title>`, meta description, and the four places the email
address appears) plus `README.md`.
